The problem

Every team deploying agents hits the same three walls: outputs nobody can verify, decisions nobody can trust, and operations nobody can prove. The industry's answer has been to add more guards to read and more logs to ignore. Dabslabs' answer is that trust is a layer — the same way TLS is a layer — and it is built, not bolted on.

What it does

One thesis, three tools: agentic systems earn trust the way people do, by showing their work. The provenance engine makes assertions checkable. The typed-decision layer makes decisions machine-readable and bounded. The governed estate makes the operations provable. Each is deployed and measured; together they are the trust layer an agentic system stands on.

The three pillars

PILLAR 1

The Provenance Engine

Agents assert things that are wrong.

Agent claims are gathered and validated against ground-truth data with natural-language inference. Every claim carries its source; what enters the graph is what survived checking, with a confidence score that means something.

Running in production: a nine-service stack verifying claims on the flagship corpus.
PILLAR 2

The Typed-Decision Layer

Agents decide without structure.

A GPU-served joint-head model scores every allowed option in one forward pass and returns the answer with a probability attached to each candidate. Out-of-range answers are impossible by construction.

112 ms p50 per decision, zero generated output tokens, 91.8% agreement against reference labels.
PILLAR 3

The Governed Estate

Agents run on operations nobody can govern.

The company's own cluster is delivered by GitOps, governed by recorded due-diligence on every consequential change, and operated by its own agents — the discipline an earlier system, Corvus, proved with signed events that scripts verified before acting.

Agent-authored pull requests and committed governance records on the live estate.

FLAGSHIP DEPLOYMENT

The verified web behind the music.

Invisible Strings

A music-lore platform where fans of any artist explore the influences, histories, and relationships woven through songs — every connection carrying its source and a confidence score. Audio is transcribed by speech recognition on an NVIDIA Jetson edge device; claims are verified by natural-language inference on RTX-class GPUs. Taylor Swift's discography is the founding corpus and the muse; the platform is artist-agnostic by construction, built for every artist after her.

Engine running in production today. Discovery designed and next. Not yet publicly launched — stated here rather than smoothed over.
